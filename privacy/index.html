Privacy

How BrainiLab uses account, gameplay and product data.

BrainiLab account and gameplay data

BrainiLab may store account profile information, game results, progression, rankings preferences, groups and personal statistics when those features are used. Detailed account statistics are private unless a player explicitly opts into a public ranking surface.

Advertising

BrainiLab is prepared to use advertising on selected non-gameplay surfaces for Free users. Advertising remains disabled until launch configuration is completed. BrainiLab+ members are entitled to an ad-free experience while their membership is active.

Payments

BrainiLab does not store full card details in its application database. BrainiLab+ checkout and subscription management are designed to be handled by Stripe.

Privacy choices

Where consent is required for advertising or related storage, BrainiLab will use a consent-management provider and expose privacy controls through the site. You can also review the Cookies page.

Launch note: before public launch, this page should be completed with the final legal entity/contact details and jurisdiction-specific disclosures.
