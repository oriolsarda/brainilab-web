Cookies & privacy choices

Browser storage and optional advertising controls used by BrainiLab.

Necessary storage

BrainiLab uses browser storage for functionality such as guest progress, session state and product preferences. Signed-in sessions may also use authentication storage required by Supabase.

Analytics and product events

BrainiLab records product events needed to understand gameplay and improve the product. Public launch analytics should be configured so that consent requirements are respected where applicable.

Advertising storage

Advertising is currently launch-gated. When enabled, BrainiLab will use a Google-certified consent-management setup for regions where consent is required before eligible advertising is served.

Manage privacy

The “Manage privacy” link in the footer is already wired as the future consent-control entry point. Until the production CMP is connected, it returns here instead of pretending that a consent dialog is active.

Launch note: finalize the CMP vendor, cookie inventory and legal contact details before enabling production advertising.
