How BrainiWord works

Guess BrainiLab’s server-selected daily five-letter English word in five attempts. After each guess, green means the letter is in the correct position, yellow means the letter appears elsewhere in the word, and dark grey means the letter is not in the answer. You can play with your physical keyboard or the on-screen keyboard.
