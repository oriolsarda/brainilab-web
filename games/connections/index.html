How Connections works

Each round shows 4–8 clues and four possible connections. Identify the one idea that links every clue. You can try again after a wrong choice, but solving in fewer attempts earns more points.

Connections and the Daily

Connections uses 20 rounds in Play Anytime and 3 rounds when selected for the Daily. “Try first” loads another full 3-round Daily-format batch with the same rules and no score.

  • 20 rounds in Play Anytime
  • 3 rounds when selected for the Daily
  • 4–8 clues per round
  • Score rewards fewer attempts
