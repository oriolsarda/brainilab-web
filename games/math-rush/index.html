What is Math Rush?

Math Rush is a fast mental-arithmetic game. You have 60 seconds to solve as many simple additions, subtractions, multiplications and exact divisions as possible. It is designed for quick daily practice and repeatable score-chasing.

How scoring works

Correct answers earn points and build a combo. A wrong answer or a skip resets the combo, but you can immediately continue to the next problem.

Math Rush and the Daily

Math Rush can appear as one of the rotating Daily games. “Try first” loads a separate full 60-second Math Rush run with the same rules and never contributes to Daily Brain Score, Health, XP or streak.
